I have always followed my passions where they have led me. I began my professional life working in animal care, from small rehabilitation facilities to large companies like Disney. I have spent over 10 years in this field, relishing the challenges that come with it. Animal care is unique among many jobs due to the versatility that is necessary to provide a high level of care. Many problems require creative and individual solutions, an aspect which has always engaged me.

Upon learning about the field of Data Science, I realized that my knack for statistics and coding could be used to explore deeper into the historical data that already existed. I have been using these skills with animal data that has been recorded over the years to find patterns and insights that are not immediately obvious to human eyes.

## Education

* BA Zoology, Michigan State University, 2011
* TripleTen Data Scientist Program, 2020
* MS Data Science, Bellevue University, 2024

## Stance on Generative AI/LLMs

While I, as a data scientist, use and manipulate machine learning and AI models, there are nuances in how I believe it should be handled.

I believe strongly in upholding ethics and human rights when it comes to physical data centers. This includes labor conditions, energy and water use, and any waste products that may be created from the center. I am against large drains on natural and human resources, and creating pollution to be passed on to humans and the natural world.

I do not believe in using AI as much as possible, but rather, strategic use of AI tools to enhance and learn, not create and automate. A person should be designing and making decisions, while AI works in a collaborative role.
