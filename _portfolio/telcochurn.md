### Churn in Telecommunications

Churn is an aspect of business that companies seek to predict and avoid. Churn is the term for customers who leave or stop using the company. This data, sourced from [IBM](https://github.com/IBM/telco-customer-churn-on-icp4d/blob/master/data/Telco-Customer-Churn.csv), contains extensive data about customers of a telecommunications company, including which customers left in the last month. This project explores the factors that may influence customers leaving, along with creating a reliable machine learning model to predict potential churn, allowing for targeted marketing programs to retain customers.
